Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 June 2026

Track doesn't collect, store, or transmit any of your data. There are no servers, no analytics, and no accounts — nothing about you ever reaches us. That's the whole policy; the rest is just spelling it out.

What we collect

Nothing. Track has no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no cookies, and no sign-up. We don't ask for an email, we don't run a server you connect to, and we have no way of knowing who uses the app or how.

Where your data lives

Track is local-first. Every timer, worklog, label, and setting is stored on your own machine as you work. It stays there. We never receive a copy, because there's nowhere for it to go.

Syncing to Jira & Tempo

When you choose to push your hours, Track sends those worklogs directly from your machine to the Jira or Tempo instance you've configured, using the credentials you provide. That data travels straight to your own Atlassian/Tempo account and is governed by their privacy terms — it never passes through us, and we never see it.

Likewise, an Excel export is a file written to your disk. What you do with it is up to you.

About this website

This page is a plain static site with no trackers, no cookies, and no analytics. For full honesty: it loads a web font from Google Fonts, and the optional “Buy me a coffee” button loads an image from Buy Me a Coffee's CDN. Like any external request, those providers may see your IP address under their own policies. App downloads are hosted on GitHub. We add no tracking on top of any of this.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we'll update this page and the date above. Since we don't have your contact details, that's the only place a change will be announced.

Questions

Five things Track does well

Lives in your menu bar

Start the clock without breaking flow

Click in, pick a Jira issue or just type a label, and the timer runs while you work. No window to babysit, no context-switch — the running task ticks away right in the menu bar.

  • One click to clock in
  • Pick an issue or free-type
  • Pause, resume, switch instantly

Jira & Tempo

Push a whole week in one click

Review your worklogs, see what's already synced, and send the rest straight to matching Jira issues or Tempo. Working offline? Export an Excel workbook and move on.

  • Maps time to the right issues
  • Tempo worklogs supported
  • Excel export when offline

Targets & focus

Know when you've done your hours

Set a daily target and Track shows the progress ring filling as the day goes. A light focus-session tally keeps long stretches honest — no forced breaks, no nagging.

  • Daily work-hour target
  • Focus-session tally
  • Calm, never naggy

Local-first

Track all week, sync when you're ready

Everything is stored on your machine as you go. Your day rebuilds into a clean timeline and weekly charts, so you can review exactly where the hours went before anything leaves your laptop.

  • Your data stays local
  • Timeline + weekly charts
  • Review before you sync

Activity & Tempo

See where the week actually went

Track rolls your time into hours-per-day against your target and a by-issue breakdown — the same numbers you push to Tempo, so your reports and your worklogs never disagree.

  • Hours per day vs. target
  • By-issue breakdown
  • Matches your Tempo worklogs
